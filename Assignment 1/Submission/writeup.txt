TASK 1 - RATING PREDICTION

Models / features          | Purpose
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
Biased SVD                 | Multiple user-book rating factor models.
SVD++ / item-side SVD++    | Implicit reading-history factors on both sides.
Collaborative neighborhoods| Cosine/Pearson and learned user/book ridge models;
                           | separate reading-presence and rating-residual terms.
CatBoost                   | Combines collaborative scores and support statistics.
Five-class neural models  | Embedding classifiers and probability combiners.

Exclude zero ratings and combine factor models with collaborative neighbors.
Train the neural combiners on historical out-of-sample component predictions.
Convert rating probabilities into their expected rating, not the winning class.
Average the selected neural and collaborative ensembles; clip outputs to [1,5].

TASK 2 - INTERACTION PREDICTION

Models / features          | Purpose
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
BPR                        | 64/96/128-factor personal preference scores.
Confidence-weighted ALS    | 128-factor implicit-feedback preferences.
EASE / restricted SLIM     | Regularized book-to-book reading relationships.
Cosine / Jaccard neighbors | Co-reading evidence and history support.
Support features           | Popularity, prior reading, user count, coverage.
CatBoost + XGBoost         | Symmetric pair classification and fixed score blend.

Treat every observed row, including rating-zero rows, as a reading interaction.
Generate historical positive/background pairs using training-popularity sampling.
Combine score differences, sums and absolute differences; train mirrored pairs.
Blend XGBoost and CatBoost margins 60/40 and choose the candidate with larger margin.

TASK 3 - RATING PREDICTION WITH TIMESTAMPS

Models / features          | Purpose
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
Temporal biases / SVD      | Calendar effects, user/item bias and preference drift.
SVD++ / temporal factors   | Implicit histories, drifting factors and residual MF.
Learned neighborhoods     | Time-weighted user/book ridge, Pearson and ordinal
                           | signals, with separate presence/residual predictors.
User-time rating scale    | Multiplicative scale on the time-dependent item bias.
128-factor temporal SVD++ | Higher-capacity implicit-history model, 200 epochs.
Local-linear smoothing    | Regularized level/slope of nearby training residuals.
CatBoost / LightGBM / XGB | Stacking plus fixed selected blends of component scores.

Exclude zero ratings and fit preference, neighborhood and temporal components.
Add user-time scales and the 128-factor model to the historical feature bank.
Fit local lines to training residuals around query times, shrinking sparse histories.
Use out-of-sample stacking and fixed selected blends; clip expected ratings to [1,5].

TASK 4 - INTERACTION PREDICTION WITH TIMESTAMPS

Models / features          | Purpose
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
BPR / ALS                  | Global and user-period preferences.
EASE / SLIM / neighbors    | Whole-history and time-local co-reading signals.
Temporal factors / biases | Calendar activity, time density and preference drift.
Factorization machine     | User/book/calendar/user-period feature interactions.
Item2Vec                   | 32-dimensional skip-gram book embeddings and
                           | timestamp-conditioned history similarities.
LightGBM                   | Combines all component-pair differences and sums.

Keep rating-zero interactions and condition collaborative scores on query time.
Fit the component models separately, then add Item2Vec similarities to the bank.
Train the selected LightGBM classifier on mirrored historical candidate pairs.
Antisymmetrize forward/reversed probabilities and output the chosen book ID.

TASK 5 - NEXT-BOOK PREDICTION

Models / features          | Purpose
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
BPR / ALS / Markov factors | Global tastes and low-rank book transitions.
FPMC variants              | Joint personal preferences and recent transitions.
PRME / TransRec / FOSSIL   | Metric, translation and high-order history signals.
EASE / neighborhoods      | Whole/recent-history co-reading and session evidence.
Order graphs / attention  | Skip transitions, two-hop paths and history attention.
Preference adaptation     | Stronger BPR and query-history factor adaptation.
Two neural combiners      | 224/260-score banks, hidden widths 64/32; equal blend.

Use reading order without numeric timestamps or rating values; retain zero rows.
Build rolling examples with the target book removed from each input history.
Train the two combiners on historical pairs with controlled background draws.
Refit components on complete histories, average the two margins and choose the book.

TASK 6 - NEXT-INTERACTION TIME GAP

Models / features          | Purpose
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
History summaries          | Gaps, quantiles, dispersion, EMA, activity and season.
Book-date / peer summaries | Candidate-book dates and leave-reader-out durations.
CatBoost MAE / log-MAE     | Conditional gap anchors, with a simple ridge component.
LightGBM / CatBoost MAE    | Balanced-history and conditional gap experts.
Median LightGBM experts   | Raw-gap median and two residual median corrections.
25/50/75% quantile models  | Predicted gap levels, spread and asymmetry features.
Augmented median experts  | Corrections using the quantile-feature bank.

Construct historical next-gap targets from earlier, penultimate and final events.
Exclude held-out events from date aggregates and exclude query readers from peers.
Fit earlier-only quantile models for historical meta features; refit for test scoring.
Combine median experts with fixed weights, clip at zero and convert days to seconds.

DATA AND VALIDATION

Rule                       | Implementation
---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
Rating-zero rows           | Excluded for Tasks 1/3; retained as observed events
                           | for Tasks 2/4/5/6, per instructor clarification.
Data sources               | Only supplied CSVs. No external metadata, scraping,
                           | ID decoding or cross-task target matching.
Available test inputs      | Use the given IDs/timestamps as task inputs; no
                           | hidden test target values are available or used.
Historical stacking        | Component fits exclude their meta-training targets.
Task 3 capacity selection | A nested base holdout inside the original fitting
                           | pool selected epochs; cached meta targets excluded.
Task 6 quantile features   | Earlier-only fits produce historical meta features;
                           | full-data fits produce test features.
Production fitting        | Fixed selected hyperparameters; no search, tuning
                           | loops or rejected models in the executable.
Output format              | Original headers, IDs and row order retained; valid
                           | candidate IDs, ratings [1,5], nonnegative gap seconds.

REPRODUCTION

Place assignment1.py in the supplied data directory, or in the sibling Submission
folder. Install the requirements and compiler listed in its initial comments.
Run python assignment1.py, or specify --data-dir, --output-dir and --tasks.
The single file contains all required supporting model source and C++ kernels.
It creates its own cache and trains from raw CSVs without previous predictions,
pretrained weights, repository checkout or network access during model fitting.
Historical fits needed for stacking remain; hyperparameter searches are omitted.
The bundled CSVs are the canonical submission files. Different compiler libraries,
BLAS implementations or package versions can change numerical details on refit.
